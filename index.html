About Me

Connecting Data Architecture, Operational Efficiency, and Strategic Execution

I am a Data Analyst and Business Analytics Operations Specialist based in Nairobi, Kenya. My work focuses on building the data infrastructure, automated pipelines, and operational workflows that enable organizations to scale efficiently and make confident, data-backed decisions. My analytical approach pairs technical rigor (SQL, Python, Power BI) with operational strategy (CRM data architecture, business process modeling, and workflow automation). Rather than delivering static, one-off reports, I design reliable data pipelines that transform fragmented data sources into centralized, decision-ready systems. Whether optimizing financial reporting cycles, analyzing workforce retention drivers, or mapping regional infrastructure data, I focus on creating sustainable data systems that eliminate operational friction and deliver immediate clarity to leadership.
