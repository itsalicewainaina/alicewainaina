About Me

I’m a Data Analyst and Business Analytics Specialist passionate about turning complex data into clear insights, efficient processes, and better business decisions.

My experience spans financial analytics, operational reporting, performance analysis, dashboard development, data quality, and workflow automation. I work with tools such as SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau to transform raw and fragmented data into structured, decision-ready information.

Beyond analysis, I’m interested in how data can improve the way organizations operate. I have worked with CRM systems, automated reporting processes, operational workflows, and performance tracking to improve visibility, reduce manual work, and help teams make more informed decisions.

I approach every project by first understanding the business problem, then using data to uncover what is happening, why it is happening, and what can be done about it. My goal is not simply to create reports or dashboards, but to build practical analytical solutions that create measurable business value.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Data Science, I’m continuously developing my technical and analytical capabilities while applying them to real-world business challenges.
